# Nobles public product guidance review — 15 October 2026

## Public website evidence reviewed

- https://mynoblescooperative.com/loans/marketlift/: MarketLift is described as short-term working capital for petty traders, with daily repayment over 25 working days and public cycle ranges of NGN 50,000-150,000.
- https://mynoblescooperative.com/loans/marketpower/: MarketPower is described as structured working capital for established traders and shop owners, with weekly repayment over 16 weeks and public cycle ranges of NGN 200,000-500,000.
- https://mynoblescooperative.com/loans/easypay/: EasyPay is described as salary-backed credit for employed members with verifiable income, monthly repayment over six months and an amount determined by approved affordability assessment.
- https://mynoblescooperative.com/: Unity Loan is described only as group-supported credit requiring cooperative-union vetting. The public site does not document an amount range or detailed repayment terms.

## Implementation boundary

The purpose catalogue is educational guidance. It helps a visitor describe a legitimate business, personal/family or group need and points to a Nobles product that may be worth discussing. It does not add an allowed lending purpose to a product policy.

A percentage, result remark or suggested plan is returned only when the selected product has effective, resolved, published PRODUCT, SCORE and ELIGIBILITY policies and the selected purpose code is explicitly present in that product's approved public-self-check contract. Missing policy continues to return POLICY_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED.

The application uses a deterministic, explainable policy calculation. It does not represent the result as artificial-intelligence approval, repayment probability, an offer, a guarantee or a final eligible amount. Formal assessment still requires membership, identity, documents, verification, affordability review and authorized human decisions.
